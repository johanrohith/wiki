Johan Rohith (born 2 May 2007) is an Indian college student pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications at Madras Christian College in Tambaram, Chennai. His interests include programming, web development, creative writing, gaming and technology.

Johan began using the internet extensively in 2018, initially through YouTube and gaming. His online interests later developed through Minecraft and Discord communities before shifting toward programming and creative work during his college years.

Early internet use and gaming

Johan began using the internet in 2018, primarily consuming YouTube content and playing games. He initially played mobile games before moving to PC gaming. During this period, gaming and YouTube were his main online interests.

He also experimented with creating gaming videos.

Minecraft and online communities

In December 2020, Johan joined the HyperLands Minecraft Bedrock community. Minecraft subsequently became a major part of his online interests, while Discord became an important platform for interacting with the wider community.

On 26 September 2021, Johan became a Discord moderator for the community. His responsibilities included moderating conversations, addressing spam and dealing with unwanted chat behaviour. His moderation period continued until approximately April 2022.

From 2021 to 2023, Minecraft and Discord were central to Johan's online activity. He also spent time with his online friends and experimented with content creation during this period.

College and programming

Johan joined Madras Christian College in 2025 and is pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications. He is currently in his second year.

During 2026, he began placing greater emphasis on programming and creative creation. This shift was partly inspired by observing his best friend develop herself and pursue her own interests. Seeing her progress encouraged him to recognise that he could similarly develop his own abilities and pursue interests he had wanted to explore since childhood.

His current learning interests include web development, javaScript, Java, GitHub and debugging. He is also learning Hindi and making his way through chess.

Projects

MCC Waste Exchange

MCC Waste Exchange is a college project designed to allow students to exchange or give away old or unwanted items so that they can be reused by others. Johan developed the website and handled the website implementation. The concept was developed through discussions with his team.

The project was a team college assignment and was completed and presented on 23 September 2026.

Campus Care

Campus Care is a college project concept for registering complaints involving issues such as electricity problems, property damage and water-related damage on campus.

Johan served as Product Owner for the project and created the presentation used to present it. The project was presented on 28 September 2026. The project is featured in the projects section of Johan's personal website.

Creative work

Echoes of the End

Echoes of the End is an original science-fiction story by Johan. Publication began on 1 June 2025. The story was published in multiple parts, but publication was left incomplete.

The work is available through his personal website at johanrohith.me.

Astrion

Astrion is a separate personal fictional concept developed by Johan after August 2026. He describes it as a concept extending beyond conventional time and universes rather than simply as the setting of a single story.

Interests

Johan's interests include programming, web development, story writing, gaming and technology. Johan has identified Minecraft as a significant game in his online history and continues to learn chess. He is also learning Hindi gradually; Tamil is his native language.

Online presence

Johan maintains a personal website at johanrohith.me, which presents public information about him and his work. His GitHub account, johanrohith, is used for coding work.

He uses Instagram for occasional life updates, X for sharing thoughts and progress, and LinkedIn as a professional profile. His YouTube account exists but is not currently an active publishing platform.
